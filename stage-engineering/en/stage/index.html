Stage technology

The stage is what the last side seat can see, what a rigger can hang and inspect, and what light can model without glaring the audience. A drawing that starts from the proscenium opening alone is incomplete.

What this gate is

The gate gathers stage design, rigging, and lighting. Design chooses among proscenium, thrust, and black box, and it insists on wings, a crossover, and a floor. Rigging separates building steel from a rigging system with a defined working load. Lighting distributes DMX, power, front light, and house light as different systems.

The rigging safety factor is defined by the applicable standard and the engineer of record. This gate does not quote a factor as if it were code text. ANSI E1.11 defines the DMX512-A link and a universe of 512 data slots. That is not a fixture count and not a power circuit. Lighting does not remove an obstruction in the sightline.

What is published now

  • Stage design

    Depth, wings, crossover, floor, and proscenium versus thrust versus black box.

  • Rigging

    Loads, fleet, who operates, inspection, and the difference between architectural steel and a rigging system.

  • Lighting

    Front-light angles, glare, DMX distribution, power, followspots, and house light versus stage light.

  • The audience constraint

    The sightline from the side seat limits useful depth. Lighting and rigging do not reverse that constraint.

When to open this gate

  • Before the tower height is frozen

    Rigging, drapes, and a cyclorama need clear height. A short tower is a scenery decision, not a later detail.

  • Before the fixtures are purchased

    The front-of-house bridge either reaches the face or it glares the audience. The fixture does not repair the bridge.

  • When the production type changes

    A black box whose audience is rearranged needs rigging points and power, not only black paint.

How to read the subject

  1. 01

    Draw the sightline from the edges of the house to the stage depth. Area that is not seen is not playing space, however deep it is on plan.

  2. 02

    Separate rigging load from the building structure. Every point has a working load from a designed system, not from how the beam looks.

  3. 03

    Distribute data and power separately. A DMX universe is 512 data slots under ANSI E1.11, a fixture may use several slots, and the electrical circuit is another item.

  4. 04

    Name who operates rigging and lighting, and where they stand. A followspot with no booth and no operator path is not handed over on opening day.

What this gate does not do

  • Depth that cannot be seen

    A deep stage cut by the sightline from the side seat. Area on paper is not a scene.

  • Rigging from a building beam

    Architectural steel is not a rated rigging system. Load and safety factor come from the standard and the engineer of record.

  • Lighting instead of a sightline

    More intensity does not let the audience see around a column or a rail. Light does not remove the obstruction.

Questions designers and owners ask

Is a black box just a black room?

No. Flexibility is rigging points, circuits, and a floor that allows the audience and the stage to be rearranged. Paint does not provide that.

Which safety factor do I adopt?

The one stated by the applicable standard and confirmed by the engineer of record. This gate does not quote a number as if it were the code.

Does 512 mean 512 fixtures?

No. Under ANSI E1.11 a universe is 512 data slots. A multi-channel fixture uses more than one, and electrical power is separate.

Where does face light go?

From the front on two angles so the eye socket is not left dark, and from a height that does not enter the audience’s eyes. An angle near 45 degrees is a planning discussion, not a law.

Is the crossover behind the drape a detail?

No. Without a crossover the performer crosses in view or does not cross. Its place is part of stage depth.

Technical sources

  • Applicable standard and engineer of record

    Rigging load, safety factor, and inspection. The gate does not replace the standard text and does not quote a numeric factor.

  • ANSI E1.11 (DMX512-A)

    The lighting data link and a universe of 512 slots. Not a fixture count and not a power guide.

  • Local building code for sightlines and exits

    Aisles, platforms, and the stage sit under the adopted code. The gate does not fix a legal width.
