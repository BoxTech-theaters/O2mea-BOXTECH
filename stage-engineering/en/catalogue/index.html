What this gate is

Two sheets do not mix. The dimension sheet has figures we show. The test sheet has a grade we do not show, because a change of fabric or foam stops the transfer. Stadium names sometimes appear in the factory file as its own references. They are not a BoxTech O2mea job list.

The BoxTech line on this page has no printed centres. The axis is fixed on the stand section. A catalogue image, including a product drawing, is not a row measurement.

What is published now

  • Stadia

    Blow-moulded HDPE, injection, and perforated polymer. ES-500, ES-100, FRESH, THOR.

  • Halls and theatres

    Upholstery and shell. ATENAS, BRISTOL, MOZART, OTELO, ECO.

  • Cinema

    Longer depth and recline. The KING RECLINER R10 sheet extends depth from 95 to 155 cm.

  • BoxTech

    Kairos, Chronos, Carmen, Gaudí. No axis figure on the card.

When to open this gate

  • Before the chair count

    The centre changes the count in the same length.

  • Before a fire claim

    Ask for the build-up report, not the family name.

  • When a stand folds

    MICRA FLEXING and Onik are named for tiers that close. Read the telescopic page.

How to read the subject

  1. 01

    Name the room: stadium, hall, cinema, hospitality.

  2. 02

    Read centres and depth. Compare them with the row width, not with the picture.

  3. 03

    Separate an exposed stadium seat from an upholstered hall seat.

  4. 04

    Return to the sightline and the aisle before the colour is adopted.

What this gate does not do

  • Copying a stadium off the factory sheet

    The reference is the factory’s. It is not a BoxTech O2mea contract.

  • An unassembled weight treated as installed

    The sheet says not assembled where it applies. Do not drop the words.

  • 13 cm read as the chair height

    On DIAMOND the cell is printed that way and the text speaks of a +13 rest. Check the drawing.

The figures are from the published sheet for that seat family: centres, depth, height, and weight. They are not a fire certificate, not a stadium capacity, and not a completed project in a client’s name. If the factory sheet names a test grade, ask for the report of the supplied build-up. Do not copy it off this card.

Questions designers and owners ask

Is the ES-500 indoor only?

The published sheet allows indoor and outdoor, including covered and VIP zones. Tip-up is a silent spring, weight is 10 kg, centres are 48–56 cm.

Do you publish a fire grade?

No. A family may be tested to UNE-EN 12727 or EN 1021 in the factory file. The grade belongs to the build-up that has the report, not to this page.

Where is Kairos?

On the BoxTech line of this page. No centres here. The axis is on the section.

Does the chair replace the screen?

No. The person who watches is seated. The screen and the projector are a separate section at the front.

Is this a price page?

No. Weight and size are not a price. The study asks about the row, not about the catalogue colour.

Technical sources

  • The published seat-family sheet

    The source of centres, depth, height and weight on the cards. Not a certificate.

  • The build-up test report

    Any fire or durability grade. Change the fabric and the transfer stops.

  • The sightline page

    C-value is a literature discussion, and the code owns the aisle. The catalogue replaces neither.
