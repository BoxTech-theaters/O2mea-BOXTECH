Client names and photographs attributed to contracts are not published here. A public case study is written only with permission. What follows are scopes that explain the method.

Reference scope: a speech hall of about 400 seats

The question is not the chair count. The section has to show the eye line to the bottom of the screen, the volume has to be discussed with mid-band RT, and the HVAC has to be named as background noise before a loudspeaker is bought. This is a teaching scope, not a named client project.

Reference scope: a theatre between speech and music

One reverberation target will disappoint one of the uses. An honest scope writes two modes, and what changes between them: a curtain, a panel, or nothing, with the compromise accepted. Stage and rigging loads go to the structure, not to a decoration item.

Reference scope: a hall whose floor must be cleared

A telescopic stand is chosen because the floor has a second use, not because the word sounds better. Ask about storage depth, track flatness, who operates the move, and inspection before each opening. Galvanizing is a corrosion subject, not a colour.
