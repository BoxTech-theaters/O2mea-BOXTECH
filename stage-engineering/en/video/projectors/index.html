Projectors

A projector is geometry first: where the lens sits, what the image width is, and what light falls on the screen from the room rather than from the projector. Throw ratio is a class name. The lens table at the shift in use is the width. A shadow in short throw is a position error, not a lamp defect.

Definition

Throw ratio = projector-to-screen distance ÷ image width. A number like a lens class does not replace that lens’s table: vertical and horizontal shift change width and place. Ambient light lifts the black floor, so a brighter projector in a lit room does not restore black. Screen gain is a figure from the screen sheet, usually a peak on axis, and it falls off axis. This page gives neither a gain nor a lumen figure.

Lamp and laser are a maintenance choice: a lamp has a stated life on the sheet, a replacement, and a cost; laser moves maintenance to cooling, filters, and the source warranty, with gradual decline rather than necessarily a sudden failure. Short throw puts the lens near the screen, so a presenter walks through the cone and casts a shadow. AVIXA DISCAS is a procedure that ties image height to the farthest viewer and the content class. This page does not perform the procedure and does not output a screen height.

When is this service needed?

  • Distance exists and light is controlled

    A hall that can be dimmed, with a screen far enough for a reasonable lens. Projection is a fit tool here.

  • Short throw is forced

    The projector is close to the screen. Shadow and shift are drawn before the lens is bought, not after.

  • Maintenance away from periodic replacement

    Long daily use may favour laser as a maintenance schedule. Not because the word is newer.

  • When a self-lit screen is more honest

    Windows that will not be covered, or a bright stand. More lumens do not build black. See the LED page.

Where it is used

  • Lecture halls

    A screen behind the talker. A ceiling throw has to clear the head and the shadow on the picture.

  • Cinema and screening rooms

    Darkness is part of the design. Gain and lens serve the end seat, not only mid-row.

  • Multipurpose halls

    House light sometimes stays on. Contrast is discussed for the lit condition, not for a laboratory room.

  • Booths and exhibitions

    Booth depth may not allow the throw. A short lens glares the aisle if it is aimed badly.

Parts of the solution

  • The lens and its table

    Throw ratio is a range, not a magic number. Width is read at the actual distance and shift.

  • The screen

    Gain, viewing angle, and material. High gain narrows the good seat and can make a hot spot.

  • Source and ambient light

    Sheet lumens in the stated lamp mode are compared with light falling on the screen from the hall. This page gives no lumen figure.

  • Mount and ceiling

    Stiffness, a cable route, and a service opening for the lamp or filter without demolishing the ceiling.

  • Image processing

    Shift, limited geometry correction, and delay against sound. Heavy geometry correction spends sharpness and does not fix a badly skewed wall.

Engineering considerations

  • Throw ratio is not the table

    Distance over width is a class. The lens table at the shift is the design.

  • Contrast is a room

    Light on the screen from windows and spotlights lifts black. The projector does not cancel that alone.

  • Gain is not free brightness

    The peak is on axis. The end seat loses it. The sheet has the curve, not only the word gain.

  • Short-throw shadow

    The cone is wide near the screen. A presenter inside it is a shadow on the picture. Place the lens above heads or outside the walk.

  • DISCAS is not performed here

    The standard ties image height to distance and task. This page does not output a height.

Materials

  • The screen surface

    Gain and look come from the screen sheet for that build. White wall paint is not a screen of known gain.

  • The light source

    A replaceable lamp or a laser source, according to the life and maintenance sheet. The choice is a schedule, not a slogan.

  • The hanging structure

    The projector’s weight and its vibration. The ceiling fixing is reviewed structurally. This page gives no weight.

Related systems

  • Hall blackout

    Curtains, windows, and house light. Without blackout, contrast is a losing decision whatever the projector.

  • Picture sound

    Lip delay against sound is checked. A projector is not necessarily instant.

  • LED as an alternative

    When light cannot be controlled or throw does not exist. The comparison is on the screen page.

  • Sightline

    The bottom of the screen may be hidden by the front row. Image height is a seating decision, not only a lens decision.

Design sequence

  1. 01

    Fix the screen position from the sightline before choosing the lens.

  2. 02

    Measure the distance available for the lens, then read the lens table at the shift, not the throw ratio alone.

  3. 03

    Estimate hall light on the screen in the real operating mode. Do not assume a darkness that will not happen.

  4. 04

    Choose lamp or laser from operating hours, replacement, and cooling, and write that into maintenance.

  5. 05

    If image size must come from the standard, run AVIXA DISCAS as a procedure, not as a guess from this page.

Delivery sequence

  1. 01

    The mount is installed so the lamp or filter service opening stays possible.

  2. 02

    Shift is set on the lens before digital correction is used. Heavy correction is the last tool.

  3. 03

    The shadow zone in short throw is marked and the presenter’s movement is kept outside it.

  4. 04

    Curtains and house light are tied to show mode, not left to random manual use.

  5. 05

    The lens table and the distance are recorded in the handover file. A lens change restarts the calculation.

Testing

  • Actual image width

    Measured on the screen at the shift in use and compared with the table. The ratio alone is not accepted.

  • Contrast in hall mode

    With operating light, not only in test darkness. A lifted black is recorded.

  • The end seat

    Screen gain is viewed from the end. A hot spot in the centre is a defect if the end is dead.

Commissioning

  • The light mode is documented

    Which curtain closes and which spotlight turns off. Handover without that mode cannot be repeated.

  • The named maintenance

    Lamp hours or laser conditions from the sheet, and a spare if it is a lamp. No slogan on this page.

  • No DISCAS height here

    If the standard is used, the result comes from the project calculation, not from this page.

Maintenance

  • Lamp replacement

    According to the sheet’s curve, not after the show has already failed. Stock is part of operation.

  • Laser filter and cooling

    Blockage cuts light and life. Maintenance is a schedule, not the sentence “maintenance free”.

  • Screen and lens cleaning

    By the allowed method. A scratched screen changes gain locally.

Common mistakes

  • Buying on lumens alone

    A lit room lifts black. A stronger projector washes the picture and does not build contrast.

  • A throw ratio with no table

    The class does not give width at the shift. The picture may grow or leave the screen.

  • Short throw in the walkway

    The presenter is a permanent shadow. The geometry was available before installation.

  • Laser as a slogan

    Choosing the source because the word is newer, with no hours, no cooling, and no warranty. Maintenance is the question.

Short comparison

The comparison is a way to choose a path. It is not a product recommendation and it is not a code.

A standard lensDistance allows the image width without extreme shift and without people walking in the cone.When distance is short, so the image grows past the screen or the projector collides with the audience.

Questions designers and owners ask

Is throw ratio enough to choose the lens?

No. It is distance over width. The lens table at the shift gives the actual width. Without it the picture can leave the screen.

Why is the picture washed out if the projector is powerful?

Hall light on the screen lifts black. Contrast is blackout and gain, not lumens alone. This page gives no lumen figure.

Does laser remove maintenance?

No. It moves part of it from lamp replacement to cooling, filters, and warranty terms. Read the sheet.

The presenter blocks the picture. What now?

Short throw puts the cone through the body. Raise the projector or move the walk out of the cone. A brighter unit does not remove the shadow.

Do you calculate screen size with DISCAS?

No. The standard exists and ties height to distance and task. This page does not perform the calculation.
