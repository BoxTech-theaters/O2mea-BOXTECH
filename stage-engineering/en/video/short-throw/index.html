Short-throw projection

A commercial cinema house is not a living room. Short throw is for home cinema, a box, and a hall with no depth behind the last row. A standard throw is for a room that has a path. The word does not mean a better picture.

Search intent

Someone putting the device above the audience because the room is short, then surprised by the shadow.

Definition

The ratio is distance ÷ width. Short means the ratio is small on that lens table, not that the device is closer to perfect. Shift is still required so the picture is not broken by digital keystone.

The closer the device is to the screen, the closer a shadow falls on a person standing in front of it. At home the device is usually above the seats and a short way from the cloth. In a university hall the path may stay long and the device may stay in a booth.

When is this service needed?

  • No depth behind the audience

    A room or a box. Not an argument for choosing it in a deep cinema house.

  • A speaker stands in front of the picture

    The shadow is drawn before the lens is bought.

  • A low ceiling

    The device may enter the sightline. The section says so.

  • After laser or lamp is settled

    The source is not the ratio. The laser page does not replace this one.

Where it is used

  • Home cinema

    Isolation from a bedroom is another question. The home-cinema page exists.

  • A hospitality box

    A few metres. Also read indoor LED if the text is small.

  • A shallow meeting room

    The device is on the nearby ceiling. The noise is in the seat.

  • Not the sunny stadium

    A short throw does not beat the sun. That is the stadium LED page.

Parts of the solution

  • A short lens from the table

    The trade name does not replace the ratio at your width.

  • The screen and the lower edge

    So a standing head does not cut it.

  • The ceiling mount

    Rigid. Vibration grows on a close picture.

  • A short clean path

    A chandelier or a beam in one metre is enough to kill the picture.

Engineering considerations

  • Shadows

    Bring the device closer and the shadow comes closer. That is the price of short throw.

  • Correction

    Optical shift first. Digital eats the edge.

  • Noise

    The device is over the head, not in a booth. The decibel sheet is the manufacturer’s, under its conditions.

  • Brightness

    No lumens here. The size is often smaller so the demand drops, and ambient still rules.

  • Not a quality grade

    Short throw is not sharper and not darker. It is geometry.

Materials

  • Screen cloth

    Gain and angle. Someone close to the device may see a hotspot if gain is high.

  • The fixing soffit

    It carries the weight from the device sheet, not from a guess.

  • Room isolation if it is a dwelling

    The projector does not isolate the neighbour. The home page separates that.

Related systems

  • Audio

    At home it may come from the screen itself. In a hall the system is separate.

  • Control

    Switch off with the room lights so the device does not stay a fan after the show.

  • LED as the alternative

    If the text is close and the light will not dim. The indoor page.

  • The seat

    A KING catalogue chair or similar is a recliner depth. It does not cut the path.

Design sequence

  1. 01

    Measure the available distance before you name the lens short.

  2. 02

    Draw the person standing in front of the screen. If they cut the picture, change the height or the type.

  3. 03

    Read the table at the real width, not at a marketing width.

  4. 04

    Put the noise above the nearest row into the brief.

  5. 05

    Do not carry lumens across from another page.

Delivery sequence

  1. 01

    The mount is fixed to structure, not to plaster alone.

  2. 02

    Shift is set and the picture is filled without heavy correction.

  3. 03

    The standing shadow is tested in a rehearsal.

  4. 04

    Noise is measured if acceptance is written.

  5. 05

    The lens sheet is handed over with the device.

Testing

  • Shadow

    A person in the presenting position. The picture stays readable or the place moves.

  • Edges

    Focus at the edges, not only the centre.

  • The near seat

    The fan is acceptable or not according to the brief.

Commissioning

  • The calculated ratio

    Compared with what was installed. The wrong lens is replaced, not digitally corrected to death.

  • The use

    Home, box, or hall. It is not handed over as a commercial cinema.

  • What is not accepted

    A catalogue picture with no real text.

Maintenance

  • Dust close to the picture

    It shows sooner because the picture is large relative to the distance.

  • The mount

    It is re-tightened. A fall onto the row is not a postponed maintenance item.

  • The lens

    It is not swapped for a longer one because the box was available. The table changes.

Common mistakes

  • Short means premium

    It is a depth solution. Premium is not the ratio.

  • Forgetting the shadow

    The speaker blocks half the slide.

  • Using it in a deep house for no reason

    A rear booth and a standard device are often clearer to maintain.

  • Mixing it with dwelling isolation

    The picture does not stop sound reaching the neighbour.

Short comparison

The comparison is a way to choose a path. It is not a product recommendation and it is not a code.

PathFits whenWeakens when
Short throwThe room depth cannot hold a path.When a standing person blocks it and the picture cannot rise. Move to LED or to a longer path.
A standard throwA booth or depth behind the audience.In a room two metres long. The device hits the back wall.
Indoor LEDNo path and no speaker shadow.When the budget or the wall opening will not take it. Return to projection.

Questions designers and owners ask

Is short throw clearer?

No. It is clearer when there is no depth and the shadow is solved. The lens adds no magical sharpness.

Does it suit a cinema house?

Rarely. A house is a longer path and a booth. Home and the box are its usual place.

Where is laser?

A separate page. A short throw can be a laser or a lamp.

Do I calculate lumens here?

No. Nor on the laser page. The sheet, the size, and the ambient.

What about the recliner?

Its depth in the catalogue can cut the lower edge. The KING RECLINER is a dimension sheet to check on the section.
