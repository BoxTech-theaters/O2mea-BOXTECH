Indoor fine-pitch LED

The hall is unfinished if the screen stays a line in another booklet. Indoor is not the stadium: less dust, a shorter distance, and house light that is quieter than the sun. The 1.5–2.5 mm band is opened for a theatre, a conference hall and a box when the seat is close. It is not a nit value and not a legal limit.

Search intent

Someone who wants a screen at the end of the hall before buying the seat apart from what it faces.

Definition

Pitch is the distance between pixel centres. The arcminute estimate says a pixel subtends about 1/60 of a degree at roughly 3.4 m per millimetre. Text may need finer than that limit. Luminance is read from the panel sheet at the hall’s content, not from an outdoor panel planted indoors.

Front or rear service is decided before the wall is closed. Heat goes to the cooling. Weight goes to the rig or to the structure, and the rigging page gives no kilogram.

When is this service needed?

  • A seat close to the picture

    A conference, a box, a stage end from which text is read.

  • House light, not sun

    Luminance is compared with the hall lighting, not with a daylight catalogue.

  • A wall that does not open every week

    If maintenance is from the rear, a gangway is required. If from the front, the tool and the spare module belong in the brief.

  • Before the last seat row is frozen

    Pitch and distance are read together. The catalogue does not come before the section.

Where it is used

  • Conference halls

    Text and slides. Finer pitch for text, not for a distant picture.

  • The stage end

    It can compete with the stage. Sightlines to the performer and to the picture are both drawn.

  • A box

    A short distance. A stadium pitch is coarse here.

  • Not the open stand ribbon

    That is the stadium LED page. Dust and sun are different.

Parts of the solution

  • The module

    Pitch from its sheet. The spare is the same pitch.

  • The processor

    Input resolution is not the wall’s resolution. The input is named.

  • The mount

    Distributed weight. The structural engineer owns the point if it is hung.

  • Cooling

    An air path the cladding does not close. Noise is a brief criterion.

  • Service

    Front or rear. The decision comes before the decor.

Engineering considerations

  • The 1.5–2.5 mm band

    A published selection band for discussing theatres, conferences and nearby boxes. Not luminance and not an obligation.

  • 3.4 m per mm

    An acuity estimate from the tangent of one arcminute. Not DISCAS and not a manufacturer rule.

  • Text is finer than a picture

    A small logo fails on a pitch that is enough for a distant film.

  • Cameras

    Moiré is tested on the real content, not on a promise in the sheet.

  • Audio is not hidden behind it

    An array or a point source needs an angle. The wall is not an acoustic curtain.

Materials

  • The panel face

    Indoor. An outdoor face in the hall glares and heats.

  • The fixing frame

    Metal to the project specification. This page names no alloy.

  • The edge finish

    The architectural frame does not steal the text pixels.

Related systems

  • Control

    The picture source and the signal path are named. This page does not design a network.

  • Lighting

    A spotlight on the face kills contrast. Stage lighting is another page.

  • Seats

    The first row and the last row are two distances. The calculator is an estimate, not acceptance.

  • Fire

    The wall’s class comes from the assembly report and the code. No class here.

Design sequence

  1. 01

    Draw the nearest and furthest seated eye before choosing pitch.

  2. 02

    Open the 1.5–2.5 mm band as a discussion if the distance is short, then leave it if the text or the distance contradicts it.

  3. 03

    Ask for the luminance sheet for the indoor environment, not a nit value from another job.

  4. 04

    Settle front or rear service, heat, and weight.

  5. 05

    Do not close the decor on a face with no spare modules.

Delivery sequence

  1. 01

    The structure is set and weighted before the first module.

  2. 02

    Modules are from the same batch and the same pitch.

  3. 03

    The air path is left as drawn.

  4. 04

    The picture is calibrated on real content: text, video, and a camera if there is one.

  5. 05

    A map of spare modules is handed over.

Testing

  • Reading from the seat

    Text at the real size from the nearest seat and the furthest.

  • Cooling noise

    In the quiet of the hall, not in the fit-out workshop.

  • Service

    One module removed by the chosen route without demolishing the face.

Commissioning

  • Content

    What will be shown, not the factory photograph.

  • Spares

    Count and place. Without that the wall is stranded at the first fault.

  • What is not accepted

    A nit value from memory, or a stadium pitch in a box.

Maintenance

  • A spare of the same pitch

    A nearby pitch is usually a different colour.

  • Indoor dust

    Lighter than a stadium and still stays on the face if left.

  • A processor update

    It can change the frame. The text is checked again.

Common mistakes

  • An outdoor panel in a conference

    It glares and heats. The environment is on the sheet.

  • Pitch taken from a reference photo

    The distance is different. The 3.4 m per mm estimate starts from your seat.

  • Closing the rear with wood

    Cooling and service die together.

  • Buying the seat before the picture

    The row faces an empty wall or a coarse pitch.

The comparison is a way to choose a path. It is not a product recommendation and it is not a code.

What this page does not claim

Questions designers and owners ask

Is 2 mm always enough?

No. It sits inside the discussion band. A close seat and small text may want finer, and a far row may accept coarser.

Is the calculator enough?

No. It is the approximate geometry. Acceptance is from the seat, the content, and the panel sheet.

Who owns the hang weight?

The engineer of record and the panel sheet. The rigging page refuses to quote a kilogram.

Does the screen isolate sound?

No. Isolation is mass and a break. Treatment is absorption. A visual wall is neither.

Where is the projector?

On its page and on the laser and short-throw pages. It is not a silent substitute for this page.

Technical sources

  • The one-arcminute estimate

    About 3.4 m per millimetre of pitch. Acuity, not a product rule.

  • The indoor panel sheet

    Pitch, luminance, power and heat under its conditions. This page copies no nit value.

  • AVIXA DISCAS

    A procedure for image size and viewing task when it is adopted. This page does not perform it.
