What this gate is

RT60 by Sabine and Eyring for surfaces, sabins alone, incoherent level addition, a seat count from area, screen geometry, throw ratio, pixel distance by arcminute, and point-source level in a free field.

Each tool has a page that explains its limit, and this gate gathers them. Material coefficients are textbook-order buttons, to be replaced by a laboratory sheet.

RT60 · SABINE

Reverberation time

Formula used: RT = 0.161 V / A. A is area × absorption coefficient, plus audience sabins. The Eyring figure is surfaces only.

0.40 sabins/person is a common order of magnitude near 500 Hz for seated audience. Published ranges are wider. Change it when the project has data.

Material figures are mid-band textbook orders of magnitude, only to try the formula. Replace them with laboratory data and the real mounting.

PIXEL PITCH

Estimated viewing distance

The distance at which a pixel subtends one arcminute is about pixel pitch in millimetres × 3.44 metres. At two arcminutes the distance is about half. This is a geometric acuity limit, not a manufacturer recommendation, and video content is often accepted closer than the pixel-blend distance.
