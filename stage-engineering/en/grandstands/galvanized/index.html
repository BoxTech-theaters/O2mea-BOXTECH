Galvanized grandstands

Hot dipping coats steel with zinc for a wet, salt, or pool-chlorine environment. Thickness comes from the galvanizer’s certificate against steel thickness in the ISO 1461 family, not from this page. Section, connection, and stability remain a design. Paint over zinc is adhesion and preparation that are agreed, not a recipe invented here.

Definition

The strategy is chosen from the environment: a dry interior may accept paint, while a coast, an open stand, or a pool hall pushes toward galvanizing or a heavier system. Galvanizing does not increase the section’s resistance and does not replace a missing connection. Cuts and holes after dipping expose steel, and their repair is named in the specification. Hollow sections need drain and vent holes so zinc can enter and air can leave safely. Hole size is coordinated with the galvanizer so the holes do not become an uncalculated section loss.

Paint over galvanizing is a duplex system. Preparation — with no chemical recipe on this page — is set between the paint maker and the galvanizer so the system sticks. ISO 12944 is a family for corrosion protection of steel by paint where it is adopted, not mixing instructions. This page declares neither a life in years nor a paint thickness. The galvanizer’s certificate is zinc thickness, not a structural conformity of the stand.

When is this service needed?

  • A coast or salt

    Salt spray on an open stand. Ordinary paint at a scratch recedes quickly.

  • A pool hall or a wet plant room

    Chlorine and humidity on the connections. The strategy is discussed before the exposed section is chosen.

  • Cutting after galvanizing is expected

    Seat holes on site. The repair method is written before steel is left bare.

  • A wish for coloured paint

    Over zinc, not over rust. The duplex system is an agreement, not a catalogue coat on a fresh dip with no preparation.

Where it is used

  • Open stands

    Rain, sun, and salt if it is present. An exposed metal seat enters the same strategy.

  • Pool halls

    An interior that is still aggressive to steel. A normal dry room is not the reference.

  • Wet telescopic frames

    The joint cracks paint. Galvanizing is discussed with the movement, not as a substitute for maintenance.

  • A dry interior

    It may not need dipping. Forcing galvanizing with no environment is cost without a real corrosion question.

Parts of the solution

  • The zinc

    A dip coating with thickness from the certificate against steel thickness. Not an extra section in the calculation.

  • Vent and drain holes

    For hollow sections. Coordinated with the galvanizer so the dip completes without an air lock and without an uncalculated weakness.

  • Cut-edge repair

    A method named in the specification for the site. A bare hole is not a postponed detail.

  • The duplex system

    Paint over zinc with its preparation agreed. This page describes neither an acid nor a roughness grade.

  • The connection

    Bolts and nuts at the same protection level, or isolated. A black connection in a galvanized frame starts the rust.

Engineering considerations

  • The coating is not the section

    Resistance and stability come from the calculation. Thicker zinc does not compensate for a thin beam.

  • The environment is named

    Coast, pool, or dry interior. The word “galvanized” with no environment does not choose the system.

  • After the dip

    Later welding or drilling breaks the coating. It is either forbidden or repaired by the named method.

  • The structural holes

    Dip venting is a shared detail. It is not left for the galvanizer to place where it weakens the moment without review.

  • No invented life

    Corrosion years depend on environment, thickness, and maintenance. This page writes no life.

Materials

  • Steel before the dip

    Grade and cleanliness from the specification. Old paint or weld slag spoils the coating.

  • The specified zinc

    Certificate thickness, not a visual guess at shine.

  • A compatible paint

    From a duplex system agreed with the galvanizer. This page names neither a solvent nor a chemical primer.

Related systems

  • The fixed or moving stand

    Galvanizing is a layer on both. The structural calculation is not replaced.

  • The metal seat

    A hole after dipping is repaired. The seat is not outside the strategy.

  • Pool and HVAC

    Chlorine and humidity are the attack. Galvanizing does not replace details that shed water.

  • Inspection

    A scratch and rust at a connection are a maintenance item. Coating does not hide the connection from the eye forever.

Design sequence

  1. 01

    Classify the environment: dry, wet, coast, pool. The strategy follows the class.

  2. 02

    Finish the section and the connection before you name the dip. Zinc is not a beam.

  3. 03

    Coordinate hollow-section holes with the galvanizer and the engineer together.

  4. 04

    If a colour is required, agree the duplex system in writing with no recipe from this page.

  5. 05

    Name the repair of site holes and cuts inside the specification.

Delivery sequence

  1. 01

    Dip after the designed welding is complete, not after every site modification.

  2. 02

    Drain holes are checked before the dip so an air lock does not burst. Execution follows the galvanizer’s instructions, not an experiment.

  3. 03

    The certificate matches the pieces. Undocumented shine is not thickness.

  4. 04

    A site hole is repaired the same day by the named method.

  5. 05

    Paint, if any, starts after the agreed preparation, not over a dip the paint maker has rejected.

Testing

  • Zinc thickness

    From a certificate to the ISO 1461 family. This page does not set the microns.

  • The repair

    Cut and hole locations are covered by the named method. The check is visual and recorded.

  • Adhesion if painted

    According to the adopted paint system, not according to a site scratch. This page describes no chemical test.

Commissioning

  • Certificates with the pieces

    Each dip batch is tied to the frame. A generic certificate with no traceability is not enough.

  • The environment is written

    Why it was dipped: coast, pool, or humidity. So maintenance knows what to inspect.

  • No life handed over from the page

    Any year estimate comes from a corrosion specialist and the environment data, not from here.

Maintenance

  • Scratches and connections

    Repaired before rust runs under the coating. The interval is a building procedure.

  • A new hole

    Immediate repair. Leaving steel bare cancels the reason for the dip at that point.

  • Repainting

    On a system compatible with the remaining zinc. Stripping to black steel restarts the question.

Common mistakes

  • Galvanized instead of calculation

    A thin section stays thin. The coating does not carry the crowd.

  • Paint with no agreement

    A coat on a fresh dip peels. The duplex system is coordination, not a site experiment.

  • Random dip holes

    They weaken the section where the moment is. The galvanizer and the engineer place them together.

  • A life in years from a catalogue

    The project environment is not the environment in the advertising sentence. This page does not fix the life.

Questions designers and owners ask

Does galvanizing strengthen the stand?

No. It is a coating. Section, connection, and stability are a calculation. ISO 1461 does not design the beam.

Do I paint over the dip immediately?

Only if the paint system and the galvanizer agree the preparation. Otherwise it peels. This page gives no recipe.

Why are there holes in the tube?

So zinc can enter and air can leave safely during the dip. The engineer reviews their place so they do not weaken the section in the wrong spot.

A site hole left the steel bright. Is that enough?

No. Bare steel starts to corrode. Repair by the method named in the specification the same day.

How many years does the coating last?

No figure here. Environment, thickness, and maintenance change the answer. The certificate is thickness, not the life of a stand.
