What this gate is

The scope is design, delivery and review for theatres, auditoriums, conference halls, acoustics, professional audio, image, seating, grandstands, hospitality boxes, cinema, stage, interiors and exhibition stands.

This edition is phase one. A page with no answer is not published. The full map is visible, and what is published is marked. The phone numbers are on the contact page, and no email is published. The brief is the message that can be answered.

What is published now

  • Reasoned knowledge

    An equation, a named standard, or an explicit limit.

  • Services without an empty slogan

    Each service says when it is needed and when it is the wrong path.

  • Preliminary calculators

    The result is for understanding, not for a tender.

  • A project method

    Without client names that were not cleared for publication.

When to open this gate

  • Before buying the product

    If the question is still about the room.

  • When two proposals conflict

    If the figures differ and the method is unnamed.

  • Not for a city search

    There is no “theatres in Riyadh” page with cloned text.

How to read the subject

  1. 01

    Read the gate, then the page that answers your question, not the reverse.

  2. 02

    Copy a number only with its source.

  3. 03

    Write the brief: use, volume, condition.

  4. 04

    Ask for measurement by the named method before handover.

What this gate does not do

  • Treating the site as a catalogue

    There is no model number and no fire certificate here.

  • Collecting city pages

    A local market is written when a local fact exists, not by swapping a name.

  • Quoting a range as a code

    90 mm or 1 second is a discussion range until the brief fixes it.

Questions designers and owners ask

The discussion is the seat inside the hall. A factory is named when its sheet is published, not as a partnership slogan.

Does the site replace a consultant?

No. It sets the questions so the meeting does not start from zero.

Where do I make contact?

From the project-study page. Copy the brief. No email is published in this edition.

Why are many pages closed?

Because 360 weak pages damage trust. Phase one is deep. The rest is a plan.

Is the content machine-translated?

Each page is written in Arabic and English as two pages, not as a skin.
