Conference hall design

A boardroom that looks finished and cannot be understood has failed. The design is speech, the microphone, the display, the hybrid participant, and isolation from the next room — not the stone on the table.

Definition

A conference room's programme is a sentence. STI as IEC 60268-16 describes it is a way to discuss how that sentence travels. RT60 as in ISO 3382-1 describes the energy that may bury it. HVAC noise raises the floor it stands on. A distant microphone in a tall ceiling is not an intelligibility plan. The display is drawn from viewing distance. The hybrid end needs a send that is not the loudspeaker mix returned to them.

If rooms sit together or are divided, isolation is a path: partition, ceiling void, floor, and door. A laboratory figure on a product is not field performance, and the page writes no STC. A room designed only as furniture and marble is the wrong scope. Unamplified drama is another scope. The page replaces neither IEC 60268-16, nor ISO 3382-1, nor the fire and egress code, and it grants no certificate for a meeting system.

When is this service needed?

  • The decision must be heard

    A meeting, interpretation, or a vote. The sentence is the product, not the photograph.

  • An end that is not in the room

    Hybrid fails when the room already cannot be understood and a camera is added.

  • Two rooms operating at once

    Speech that crosses is an isolation failure, not bad manners. It is designed before the operable wall.

  • Wrong scope

    A marble and furniture board with no speech criterion, or a theatre, or a cinema. Beauty alone does not enter this page.

Where it is used

  • A single boardroom

    A small volume tempts people to ignore RT60. A reflective table and a hard ceiling are enough to stretch the sentence.

  • A plenary with a platform

    Distance to the last row brings rake and view back, and speech remains the programme.

  • A divisible hall

    Each mode — open, half, or third — has a criterion. A partition with no seal does not create the modes.

  • Adjacent interpretation rooms

    Isolation and a close microphone are a working condition, not a luxury. The void above the ceiling is closed.

Parts of the solution

  • Speech source

    The speaker's mouth, and a microphone at a known distance. A distant ceiling is a noise source more than a sentence source.

  • The room as a channel

    Volume, absorption, and HVAC noise. Written as criteria, not as swatches on a board.

  • The display

    Height, distance, and window glare. A large logo on the wall is not a viewing distance.

  • The hybrid path

    A send microphone, a local loudspeaker, and echo control. Mixing them on one bus returns the far end to itself.

  • The isolation boundary

    Wall, ceiling, floor, and door. Drawn as a continuous line, not as a product name.

Engineering considerations

  • Intelligibility is a criterion, not an impression

    STI is discussed under IEC 60268-16. The pass mark is in the brief. The page grants no badge.

  • A shorter RT60 for speech

    Discussed as shorter than a music room wants. The seconds are a project criterion measured by a stated method.

  • Noise is a curve

    NC or NR is a possible brief language. The page does not publish the curve number as a fact of the room.

  • The partition is not the whole isolation

    Flanking above and below defeats a laboratory report. No STC is written here.

  • Glass and privacy

    A glass front on a public corridor exposes speech. If privacy is required, it is a design item.

Materials

  • Table and ceiling surfaces

    A reflector close to the microphone stretches the syllable. Treatment goes where measurement points, not where the board looks empty.

  • Partition and seals

    Mass, or two leaves, with seals at the edges. The word acoustic on a catalogue does not close a void.

  • Finish with no class from this page

    Fire performance comes from the product report and the adopted code. We do not carry a class into the specification.

Related systems

  • Microphones

    Gooseneck, lapel, or ceiling: each has a different distance and gain. The choice follows the room.

  • Speech loudspeakers

    A nearby Point Source or a distributed ceiling follows the length. A Line Array in a short room is usually an overstated scope.

  • Display

    LED or projection after distance and glare are stable. The page does not choose the pixel pitch.

  • Hearing loop

    If access is required it is drawn with the metal in the floor. It is not left until after the carpet.

Design sequence

  1. 01

    Write who must understand the sentence: the last seat, an interpreter, and a remote end.

  2. 02

    Tie microphone type to room volume and ceiling height before choosing the loudspeaker.

  3. 03

    Place the display on viewing distance and glare control, not on a logo axis.

  4. 04

    Draw the isolation boundary as continuous in every mode of a divisible room, including above the ceiling.

  5. 05

    Write STI, RT60, and the noise curve as brief criteria. Do not copy a score from this page.

Delivery sequence

  1. 01

    Fix the void above the partition before the suspended ceiling closes.

  2. 02

    Run microphone, display, and loop routes before the final finish.

  3. 03

    Install the seal with the door, not as a later item after the complaint.

  4. 04

    Balance HVAC in meeting mode before any acceptance measurement.

  5. 05

    Test hybrid with a real send, not by playing music through the loudspeaker.

Testing

  • STI at seating positions

    To IEC 60268-16 and the named method, with the meeting microphone on if it is part of the design.

  • Noise in operating mode

    HVAC as in a meeting. The page sets no NC figure for acceptance.

  • Leakage between rooms if required

    By the method written in the brief. It is not replaced by an STC report for the partition product alone.

Commissioning

  • A sentence understood from the last seat

    A functional check with the measurement, not instead of it: names and numbers, not the word clear.

  • A far end repeats the test

    They hear the sentence and do not hear their own voice as echo. That failure is closed before handover.

  • No system certificate

    The record describes the measurement. This page does not accredit a meeting platform.

Maintenance

  • A ceiling opened after handover

    It reopens a void above the partition. Isolation is closed again at every cabling visit.

  • A microphone replaced by a more distant type

    It changes gain and noise. The old STI does not remain valid by assumption.

  • The door seal

    It wears and passes speech into the corridor. Checking it is functional maintenance.

Common mistakes

  • A room for photography only

    Wrong scope: marble and a table with no speech criterion. Handsome and unintelligible is a failure.

  • A camera on a failed room

    Hybrid does not repair a room the people sitting in it cannot understand.

  • A partition with no ceiling

    A shared void above the rooms cancels the name of the acoustic partition.

  • A Line Array as a default

    A short room may need a point source or a distributed ceiling. Cabinet size is not the design.

What this page does not claim

No fire classification, absorption coefficient, sound pressure level, or legal capacity on this page may be copied into a quotation. Those values come from a product test report, a measurement, or the local code adopted for the project.

Questions designers and owners ask

Why does a handsome room fail?

Because the programme is speech. RT60, HVAC, or a distant microphone buries the sentence. Finish does not replace a criterion that was never written. Stopping at marble is the wrong scope.

If I add a camera, is the meeting hybrid?

No. The far end needs a send microphone and a room that does not return their voice as echo. A camera on a room that cannot be understood broadcasts the failure.

Is the partition STC enough?

The page gives no STC. Even a laboratory report is not enough if the void above the ceiling or below the floor stays open.

What does ISO 3382-1 have to do with a meeting room?

It is a measurement reference for performance spaces. Using it here is stated in the brief if the scope differs. The page does not replace it and does not invent RT60 seconds.

When is this page the wrong scope?

When the ask is unamplified drama, a cinema, or a furniture supply only. Open the matching gate instead of naming the room a conference.

Technical sources

  • IEC 60268-16

    Objective rating of speech intelligibility by STI. This page does not replace the standard and does not set the project score.

  • ISO 3382-1

    Measurement of room parameters. Whether it applies to a meeting room is stated in the brief. The page does not replace the document.

  • The building, fire, and egress code adopted by the project

    Occupancy, exits, and finish classification. The page names no city and does not carry a product fire class.
