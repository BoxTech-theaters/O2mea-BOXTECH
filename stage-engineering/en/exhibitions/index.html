Exhibition booths

A booth has a short life and is still a building: it stands, stores samples, lights the product under the hall’s real light, shows a screen from a distance a visitor can read, then comes apart. The render fixes the brand, not the joint.

Search intent

An exhibitor who needs a stand that assembles inside the hall’s window and strikes without the store living in the aisle.

What this gate is

This gate introduces booth design as a temporary structure inside a hall that has height, loading, and rigging rules. The design includes closed storage, lighting, the declared power, LED viewing distance or projector throw, and move-in and move-out hours.

Pixel pitch is discussed with the one-arcminute estimate, not a brand rule, and AVIXA DISCAS exists for image size and is not performed here. No invented luminance, no manufacturer partner, and no fire certificate copied from the render. If a class is required, its source is the test report of the materials as built.

What is published now

  • Booth design

    Brand, storage, lighting, power, a screen, assembly hours, and dismantling. Not a render alone.

  • Hall rules

    A height limit, a rigging ban, a floor load, and a neighbour on the line. These are site constraints, not style options.

  • The image in the aisle

    A short viewing distance. Pitch and throw ratio are chosen for that depth, not for the studio image.

  • After the show

    The strike path, the crates, and reuse if the booth will return. A joint that must be welded is not a booth.

When to open this gate

  • Before the floor space is hired

    Depth decides projector throw or screen legibility. A narrow plot does not fit an idea designed for another hall.

  • When the hall manual arrives

    Height, power, build hours, and a rigging ban. The manual beats the render where they conflict.

  • Before the graphic is printed

    Storage and joints consume a face. Printing a full wall and cutting a door late spoils both brand and structure.

How to read the subject

  1. 01

    Start from the hall constraint: metres, hours, available amperes, and whether rigging is allowed. Then design what can be built inside that constraint.

  2. 02

    Assign a closed volume for storage. If it does not exist, the aisle becomes the store on opening day.

  3. 03

    Check screen distance against pixel pitch or the lens table. The one-arcminute estimate is a geometric guess, and DISCAS is not performed on this gate.

  4. 04

    Design the strike with the assembly. Part count, tools, and who owns the crates after closing are part of the drawing.

What this gate does not do

  • A render with no joint

    A form that cannot be assembled in the hall’s hours or struck without breaking it. A booth is a build method, not a view.

  • A screen with no distance

    An LED wall that looks smooth in the drawing shows its pixels from a two-metre aisle. Pitch is chosen for the distance.

  • Power guessed on site

    Screens, lighting, and hospitality are summed before the declaration to the hall. The breaker does not absorb a surprise.

Questions designers and owners ask

Is a booth furniture?

In behaviour it is a short-life building: stability, an exit inside the plot if it is large, power, and sometimes a materials class. The hall manual and the local code state the limit.

Where do I take screen brightness?

From the manufacturer sheet. The hall may be bright. This gate gives no nit value.

Do I hang from the hall roof?

Only if the manual and a rated rigging system allow it. Many halls forbid hanging from the building structure. The render does not hold that permission.

Where does storage go?

Inside the booth, sized for samples and the crates you need during the day. Storage in the aisle usually breaks both your operation and the organiser’s rules.

What do I open for the detail?

The booth-design page, then the LED and projector pages if there is an image, then the hall manual itself.

Technical sources

  • The organiser’s hall manual

    Height, hours, power, and rigging. This gate does not replace the contract conditions with the hall.

  • AVIXA DISCAS and the display sheet

    Image size from the standard when it is required, luminance and pitch from the manufacturer. The gate neither performs the calculation nor publishes a nit value.

  • Material test report and local code

    Any fire class or floor load comes from the report and the adopted code. There is no certificate on this gate.
