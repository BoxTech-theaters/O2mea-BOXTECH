Audiovisual (AV) systems: sound, display and control for theatres and halls

Audiovisual systems carry content from the platform to every seat and from the room to the world. Yet the best equipment cannot rescue a room with poor acoustics or loudspeakers in the wrong places. At BoxTech O2mea we design AV systems together with the room and choose equipment by real use rather than brand names.

Consult on WhatsApp0541102224

How we approach av systems

Sound design starts with coverage: how does sound reach the first and the last row at a similar level? Here we choose between line arrays for long rooms and theatres and point-source or distributed loudspeakers for smaller rooms and conference halls, using DSP to set delays and balance.

Display depends on content size, distance and ambient light: LED walls for lit rooms and events, projectors for darkened rooms and cinemas, sized so text is legible from the furthest seat.

Control brings everything into one interface and is now built on networks that carry audio, video and control, making recording, streaming and later expansion easier.

Engineering aspects

Speech intelligibility

We target a high STI, which depends on room reverberation and noise.

Coverage uniformity

Limited level variation between seats.

Screen size

Following industry guidance such as AVIXA viewing-distance standards.

Networks

Networked audio protocols such as Dante for flexible distribution.

Checklist before you start

  • Are room acoustics suitable before choosing equipment?
  • Is simulated coverage even?
  • Are screen size and brightness right for ambient light?
  • Is infrastructure (power, networks, containment) planned with the building?
  • Is there safe maintenance access?
  • Are training and operating documents in scope?

Common mistakes we help you avoid

  • Buying powerful equipment for a highly reverberant room
  • Loudspeakers positioned to cause feedback with microphones
  • Neglecting earthing and dedicated circuits
  • No documentation after handover

Requirements in Saudi Arabia

We supply equipment from authorised suppliers with warranty documentation, address electrical and suspended-load safety, and hand over as-built system drawings to ease maintenance and expansion.

How to prepare your request

To speed up the study, prepare what you have: the architectural plan (PDF or CAD), building sections and clear heights, the expected audience size, the main uses in order of importance, photos of the existing site if any, and the target programme. The information need not be complete; helping you complete it is part of our work.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a line array and a point source?

A line array controls vertical dispersion to cover long distances evenly; a point source suits smaller rooms and shorter throws. Our knowledge hub explains the difference.

Is an LED wall better than a projector?

In lit rooms and events usually yes; in darkened rooms a projector remains a good economical option.

Can the hall stream events?

Yes, with cameras, audio feeds and a suitable network.

Do you offer maintenance contracts?

Yes, periodic maintenance and technical support can be agreed.
