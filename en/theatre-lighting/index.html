Theatre lighting: positions, fixtures, control and power for theatres and halls

Stage lighting creates what the audience sees: where they look, how they read a performer’s face, and when the mood changes. Before fixtures, though, it needs the right positions in the building: front-of-house bridges at a suitable angle, bars over the stage, side booms, and power and control routes. At BoxTech O2mea we design lighting as part of the building, not as an afterthought.

How we approach theatre lighting

We start with front-of-house positions because they are the hardest to add later. The classic approach sets front light at an elevation of roughly 45° to the performer, from two sides, to light the face without harsh shadows, and that fixes where ceiling bridges must go.

We then distribute top, back and side light over the stage to give depth and separation from the background. Fixtures are chosen by throw distance, beam angle and production type, with a clear move today towards LED for lower heat and consumption.

Control is the backbone: DMX or an Ethernet lighting network, enough outlets around the stage, and a console your team can operate. House lighting that dims smoothly, aisle lighting and emergency lighting complete the system.

Engineering aspects

Front-light angle

About 45° vertical to balance facial light and shadow length.

Control

DMX512 and sACN or Art-Net for larger systems.

Power

Dedicated circuits and good earthing to avoid audio interference.

Heat and noise

Low-noise fixtures that do not mask natural sound.

Checklist before you start

  • Are room acoustics suitable before choosing equipment?
  • Is simulated coverage even?
  • Are screen size and brightness right for ambient light?
  • Is infrastructure (power, networks, containment) planned with the building?
  • Is there safe maintenance access?
  • Are training and operating documents in scope?

Common mistakes we help you avoid

  • Buying powerful equipment for a highly reverberant room
  • Loudspeakers positioned to cause feedback with microphones
  • Neglecting earthing and dedicated circuits
  • No documentation after handover

Requirements in Saudi Arabia

We supply equipment from authorised suppliers with warranty documentation, address electrical and suspended-load safety, and hand over as-built system drawings to ease maintenance and expansion.

How to prepare your request

To speed up the study, prepare what you have: the architectural plan (PDF or CAD), building sections and clear heights, the expected audience size, the main uses in order of importance, photos of the existing site if any, and the target programme. The information need not be complete; helping you complete it is part of our work.

Frequently asked questions

Is LED suitable for theatre?

Yes. Most modern stage fixtures are LED, with low heat and colour control. The key is good colour quality and smooth dimming.

Can stage lighting be added to an existing hall?

Yes, but front-of-house positions may need structural solutions such as bridges or suspended bars.

Who operates the system?

We scale the system to your team and train them, and can prepare ready scenes for recurring uses.

Is house lighting included?

Yes. Dimmable audience lighting, step lighting and emergency lighting are part of the design.
