Glossary of theatre, hall and acoustics terms

18 essential terms in Arabic and English.

Terms

TermEnglishShort definition
AuditoriumAuditoriumA large audience room designed for listening and viewing, used for lectures, conferences, concerts and ceremonies.
TheatreTheatreA building or room designed for live performance on a stage before an audience, with stage lighting, flying and wings.
Acoustic TreatmentAcoustic TreatmentImproving sound quality inside a room by controlling reverberation, echo and reflections with absorptive, diffusive and reflective materials.
SoundproofingSoundproofingStopping sound travelling between rooms or between inside and outside through mass, decoupling and sealing.
RT60RT60The time sound in a room takes to decay by 60 dB after the source stops.
ReverberationReverberationThe persistence of sound in a room after the source stops, caused by many closely spaced reflections from surfaces.
SightlineSightlineThe imaginary line from a viewer’s eye to a defined point on stage or screen, which must not be blocked by the head in front.
RiserRiserThe raised step or platform on which a row of seats is fixed in a tiered hall.
StageStageThe area where performers present the show, with its wings, flying, floor and related services.
ProsceniumProsceniumThe architectural frame separating stage from house, forming the “picture frame” through which the audience watches.
Auditorium SeatingAuditorium SeatingFixed or flexible seats for auditoriums, lecture and conference halls, often with writing tablets and built for daily use.
Theatre SeatingTheatre SeatingAudience seats in theatres, designed for comfort over long performances, acoustic performance and safety.
Acoustic PanelAcoustic PanelA panel fixed to a wall or ceiling to absorb or diffuse sound, reducing reverberation and echo.
Acoustic WallAcoustic WallA wall designed for a specific acoustic role: treatment (absorbing, diffusing or reflecting) or isolation (stopping transfer).
Acoustic CeilingAcoustic CeilingA ceiling designed for an acoustic role: reflective to direct sound to the audience, absorptive to reduce reverberation, or isolating against noise from above.
AV (Audiovisual)AV (Audiovisual)The set of sound, image and control systems: loudspeakers, microphones, processors, displays, projectors, cameras and control.
Performing Arts CentrePerforming Arts CentreA building housing several live-performance spaces such as a main theatre, studio and concert hall, with audience and artist services.
Conference HallConference HallA hall designed for meetings, conferences and seminars, prioritising speech clarity, visuals, interaction and streaming.
