Exhibition and event halls: flexible spaces with controlled sound and quick set-up

An exhibition hall can change in one week from a trade show to a conference to a concert. That flexibility needs strong infrastructure: rigging points, distributed power, networks and acoustic treatment that stops a large hall turning into echo. At BoxTech O2mea we design and build what makes these halls convert quickly and safely.

Consult on WhatsApp0541102224

How we approach exhibition halls

Large halls with high metal roofs often suffer from very long reverberation. Treating the ceiling with absorbers or baffles lowers general noise and helps sound systems during conferences and shows.

Each event has its needs: a platform or temporary stage, mobile grandstands, exhibitor stands and screens. We deliver these with modular systems that speed up build and strike.

With the venue management we plan approved rigging points and loads, power and network panels, and freight routes inside the hall.

Checklist before you start

  • Are required spaces and their adjacencies defined?
  • Is isolation planned between simultaneously used spaces?
  • Are audience, artist and freight routes separated?
  • Is infrastructure flexible for changing events?
  • Are the operator’s requirements clear?
  • Is an operation and maintenance plan part of the project?

Common mistakes we help you avoid

  • Placing sensitive halls next to the loading dock or plant
  • Not coordinating early with the exhibition designer or operator
  • Temporary systems without approved rigging points
  • Neglecting accessibility

Requirements in Saudi Arabia

Cultural centres, museums and exhibition halls in the Kingdom receive large, diverse audiences, so we address safety, egress and accessibility requirements and coordinate with the operator and with content and conservation specialists as the project requires.

What you receive

  • An initial technical notes report after drawing review or site visit
  • A clear itemised scope of work
  • Approved drawings and specifications before construction
  • A programme linked to project stages
  • Records of concealed works and handover with documents and training

How to prepare your request

To speed up the study, prepare what you have: the architectural plan (PDF or CAD), building sections and clear heights, the expected audience size, the main uses in order of importance, photos of the existing site if any, and the target programme. The information need not be complete; helping you complete it is part of our work.

Frequently asked questions

How do we reduce echo in a large exhibition hall?

Usually by treating the ceiling with suspended baffles or absorptive panels, as it is the largest available surface.

Do you build exhibition stands?

Yes, drawing on the site’s dedicated stands section.

Are mobile grandstands safe?

Yes, when installed to the manufacturer’s instructions and inspected before the event.

Can the hall be divided?

Yes, with operable walls and independent sound systems.
