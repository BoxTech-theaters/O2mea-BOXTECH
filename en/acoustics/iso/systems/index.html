BoxTech O2mea/Acoustics/BoxTech O2mea iso reference/BoxTech O2mea iso systems

Catalogue

Systems with names, not ‘various absorbers’.

Each family has its own build, and a certificate that does not travel to its neighbour. Euro samples are for professionals under the European shop terms; a project outside that is quoted.

  • Section

    Ceiling absorbers

    Fixed direct to the soffit or hung on wires. The families are board, frame, skin, cover and metal.

  • Section

    Baffles

    Vertical blades. One colour or two, rounded or sharp, framed or not. The families are board and frame.

  • Section

    Wall absorbers

    Five families on the wall: board, frame, skin, cover and metal. Fixing is direct, and the effect is inside the room.

  • Section

    Partition walls

    They divide a large room into hearing zones without building an insulating wall. The families are board and frame.

  • Section

    Table absorbers

    Screens between desk groups. They shelter a nearby voice. They do not treat the hall ceiling. board and frame.

  • Section

    Individual absorbers

    A free shape: round, square or polygonal. Open-pored polyester, and textile faces.

  • Section

    CAS edition

    Absorbers planned with modular CAS rooms: cover, board and wings. Demountable and reusable.

  • Section

    Design absorbers

    The wings® family: mini, midi and maxi, then aura with backlighting and glow as a light.

  • Section

    Trade samples

    Sample prices published for professionals in the European trade shop. They are not project supply prices.

  • 01

    BOXTECH ISO board

    The classic

    A frameless polyester ceiling absorber in 24 or 40 mm. Hung on wires or fixed direct, with a quiet surface.

    Material
    Polyester, up to 95% recyclable
    Thickness
    24 mm and 40 mm

    Sample from €22.95

  • 02

    BOXTECH ISO frame

    The creative

    A fabric-covered absorber in a visible frame with softly rounded corners. Frame and insert are chosen separately.

    Build
    Fabric plus a visible frame
    Edge
    Rounded corners, patented look

    Sample from €29.70

  • 03

    BOXTECH ISO skin

    The elegant

    Cloth pulled over every edge, so the face reads as one frameless plane. The quiet is in what is absent.

    Build
    Cloth over the edge, frameless
    Effect
    Homogeneous face, softer tone

    Sample from €29.70

  • 04

    BOXTECH ISO cover

    The striking

    An aluminium frame and a cloth tensioned by a patented process. Stiffer build, clearer edge. This is the textile family Lindner describes as building-material class A1.

    Build
    Aluminium frame + tensioned cloth
    Fire
    Class A1 stated for this textile family

    Sample from €39.15

  • 05

    BOXTECH ISO metal

    The specialist

    Metal absorbers: broadband units for the whole range, and bass units when the low end dominates.

    Types
    Broadband, and bass absorbers for low frequencies
    Material
    Metal, not textile

  • 06

    BOXTECH ISO baffle

    The suspended line

    Vertical fins on the ceiling. Both faces work, and the soffit stays open for services and light. glowline adds a line of light between the pair.

    Working faces
    Two faces
    Design
    One or two colours · sharp or rounded · framed or not

  • 07

    BOXTECH ISO wings®

    The designed object

    Contours hung as an architectural object. Sound is treated, and the wall does not become a grid of rectangles.

    Idea
    Form plus absorption, not a repeated tile
    Custom
    Contours and materials per project

  • 08

    BOXTECH ISO partitions

    The room dividers

    Freestanding screens that divide a large room into hearing zones, without building a wall.

    Role
    Light acoustic and visual zoning
    Limit
    Not a sound-insulating wall

  • 09

    BOXTECH ISO desk

    The desk screen

    Felt screens between desks. They shelter a nearby voice from the next seat; they do not treat the hall ceiling.

    Range
    Local, between seats
    Does not replace
    Ceiling treatment in an open volume

  • 10

    BOXTECH ISO CAS Edition

    The whole room

    An edition that completes CAS room concepts: the absorber is planned with the room, not stuck on afterwards.

    Scope
    Complements CAS room concepts
    Supply
    With the room, not after it
