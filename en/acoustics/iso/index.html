Read this before choosing

Four rooms. Each has a line that is not borrowed from its neighbour.

The catalogue chooses the absorber. The atlas names the insulation limit. Mixing them is what makes a specification thin: a class A panel on a hall ceiling offered as if it were a theatre wall. The figures here are planning ranges, checked against the code and a measurement. They are not laboratory certificates.

A theatre, about 600 seats

The audience and the player share one volume. DnT,w is not written between them. It is written towards the foyer: a two-door lobby, not both open during a quiet scene. It is written towards a room under the pit: L'nT,w first, because the drum is impact, then airborne sound if that room is used while the orchestra plays. Control glass is two panes of unequal thickness and a frame with no drain slot towards the house. A class A ceiling panel comes after that, to shorten the tail. Its figure is not pasted onto the house wall.

Language of the schedule

Six sentences for the schedule. And what is not written in their place.

This is planning language for a specifier, not a product certificate. The catalogue names the absorber. These sentences name the insulation limit, so a panel is not bought and handed over as if it were a wall.

  1. 01

    Absorption inside the room

    Supply a BOXTECH ISO absorber by its system name, thickness, position and area, with the absorption class published for that system, not for the whole family. The target is an approximate reverberation time, checked on the calculator and then by measurement. The line does not mention Rw or DnT,w.

    Do not write: “acoustic insulation, class A.” Class A is absorption. Insulation is a different figure for a different element.

  2. 02

    Theatre house towards the foyer

    DnT,w is measured between house and foyer with doors closed, under ISO 16283-1. The entrance is a lobby of two doors, not both open during a quiet scene. A single leaf’s Rw is not accepted in place of measuring the two rooms. The pit is not a party to this line: it is open to the seats on purpose.

    Do not write insulation between the audience and the player. They share one volume. A ceiling panel shortens the tail and does not close the foyer.

  3. 03

    Two halls and a moving wall

    DnT,w is measured after the operable wall is installed, not from the panel’s sheet while it is still on the floor. The ceiling above the track is closed to the slab. If the hall sits under bedrooms, add L'nT,w after the finished floor: mass on a resilient layer, and a perimeter strip that does not touch the wall. Reinforced music below 125 Hz is named on its own, because a single number hides it.

    Do not replace a floating floor with an absorbing ceiling panel. Impact climbs the slab. Absorption stays with the guests inside the hall.

  4. 04

    A stadium suite

    Three separate lines. The bowl: reverberation and layout, with no DnT,w between the crowd and the pitch. The suite: installed glass and a door, measured as DnT,w with the crowd in the bowl. The neighbour: the permit limit at the façade, and Ctr if the spectrum is low. Jumping on the stands is a fourth, impact line if the frame reaches the suite floor. Rain on a light roof is a fifth line for the commentary room.

    Do not fold the three into “stadium insulation, 50 dB.” The bowl is not insulated from itself, and the suite is not measured by the neighbour’s window.

  5. 05

    A speech classroom beside music

    The two rooms should not touch if the plan allows it. If they do, the music limit is higher than a speech-classroom limit, the door is a complete set, and the duct is not shared. A suspended ceiling is closed to the slab above the partition. After the shell is closed, an absorber from the catalogue is specified for an area that can bring reverberation toward the school guide, often about 0.6 seconds for an ordinary speech room. That is a planning target, not a panel certificate.

    Do not promise a volume without a door the insulation of a closed classroom. An open corridor is a door. A practice room is not a classroom that gets the same panel.

  6. 06

    Handover, not opening night

    Insulation is handed over by a field measurement: ISO 16283-1 for airborne sound and ISO 16283-2 for impact, in third octaves, with doors and services as they will be in use. Absorption is handed over by a reverberation measurement in the same room after installation. An impression on opening night is not a record.

    Do not accept a laboratory certificate for a door specimen and then measure the whole room expecting the same figure. A small hole caps a large wall.

The material, without generalising

Polyester named by its sheet, not by a green sentence.

board is described as up to 95% recyclable polyester. The S24/R24-C sheet adds absorption class A, mineral-fibre free, Oeko-Tex® Standard 100, Indoor Air Comfort GOLD, and fully recyclable. cover is another family: an aluminium frame, and a textile Lindner describes as building-material class A1.
