Insulation atlas/Hall · divide

An operable wall is a system: track, pocket, ceiling and floor seal. Not a panel.

Two exam halls, or a wedding beside a quiet room, fail in the ceiling above the track more often than in the panel catalogue.

Where the figure falls

The laboratory tests the leaf with its track in a controlled opening. The site adds a suspended ceiling that runs over the track, a pocket where the panels fold and stay open to both halls, a floor the seal does not reach because the carpet is thick, and an escape door in the middle of the wall. Any one of these beats the leaf. The line in the schedule is a field DnT,w after installation and closing, not the catalogue Rw. Anyone who wants two independent halls carries a barrier from slab to slab above the track, closes the pocket with a door, and fixes the floor build-up before ordering the seal.

  • A hotel ballroom: dancing is impact to the rooms above, and the operable wall is airborne sound to the next hall. Two figures.
  • Two simultaneous exams are not run on a partition whose ceiling was never closed.
  • After insulation, variable absorption inside each half sets speech or music. It is not used instead of the partition.

Fixed walls around the hall

The wall to the foyer or to the kitchen is sometimes a heavier duty than the operable wall, because the kitchen and the corridor do not stop. A double wall with a cavity and a quilt, no common screw, and no rigid duct, beats a single heavy wall when the source runs continuously. The service door from the kitchen is treated as an isolating set, with a lobby if washing and metal run during service.
