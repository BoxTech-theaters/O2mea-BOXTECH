Insulation atlas/Measurement

The measurement day is agreed before the build, not after the complaint.

ISO 16283-1 is airborne sound in the building, ISO 717-1 the weighting. The laboratory is ISO 10140, which is something else. The contract names which.

What is measured

DnT,w is a level difference standardised to the receiving room’s reverberation time. It suits a theatre and a foyer, or a hall and a corridor, where the volumes differ. R'w describes the wall in the building, flanking included. Rw remains a laboratory specimen. Impact is L'nT,w in the room below. Background NR is measured with the air system at the agreed speed. The sample of partitions is named in the contract: not the kindest room, and not the worst corner alone if an average of positions was agreed.

  • At least three source positions in large rooms.
  • Doors closed as in operation; permanent openings stay as they are used.
  • The curve is delivered with the single number so a weakness at 125 Hz is visible.
  • Who rebuilds if the partition falls short is named before signature.

What is not a measurement

A phone app, a handclap, and the sentence ‘it seems quiet’ are not ISO 16283. The calculator on this site is Sabine for reverberation time, not a prediction of DnT,w. ISO 12354 is a design prediction before the pour. Handover is a measurement. BOXTECH ISO does not issue a laboratory certificate from these pages.
