Insulation atlas/Hall · hearings

A hearing room isolates intelligible speech from the corridor and from the next room.

Clarity inside the room is absorption and quiet air-conditioning. Stopping speech leaving is insulation. Mixing them exposes a file.

Two limits

Inside the room, a short reverberation time and a low background so testimony is understood without a loud loudspeaker. That is not Rw. Towards the corridor and towards a waiting room or a retiring room, DnT,w, usually with paired doors, because the waiting room is noisy and the hearing is sometimes quiet. Public glass, if it exists, is an isolating element, not décor. A shared duct between two hearing rooms is a repeated mistake. Do not promise a laboratory door figure and then measure the room with an open vision panel.
