Theatres and halls in Dammam, Khobar and the Eastern Province

The Eastern Province is a major industrial and commercial centre, with demand for corporate, training and conference halls alongside universities, schools and cultural centres.

Corporate and training halls

Many projects in the region are corporate training and conference halls that need high speech clarity, display and recording systems and sometimes flexible division with operable walls.

Climate

Coastal humidity combined with heat calls for the same care as Jeddah in material choice, plus quiet HVAC running at high loads.

How we serve

Remote drawing review, scheduled visits and construction follow-up at critical stages, with an installation team travelling to the project programme.
