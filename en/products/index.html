The seat catalogue

One chair does not belong in a stadium, a theatre and a cinema at once. Choose the room, then the model, and check the centre on the drawing before the count.

  • General admissionKairosThe hallseating general-admission stadium seat. Polymer, tip-up, centres locked to the sector.
  • DensityChronosPolymer for a dense bowl. One maintenance regime when the count is in the thousands.
  • Narrow arenaOnikA short upright depth for arenas and telescopic stands so the system can close.
  • Arena suiteOnik+The hospitality Onik. A fuller back, and harder use than a quiet theatre seat.
  • StadiumAmikStadium family 310, 312 and 313. Chosen by sector, not by the photograph alone.
  • ArenaArena 302A multi-use arena seat. hallseating also places it in education halls.
  • BowlStadiumThe classic stadium line in the catalogue, beside Kairos and Chronos.
  • TheatreCarmenAn upholstered playhouse seat. The action is judged by silence, not by bowl speed.
  • HallGaudíA hall seat with a clearer identity, when the form is part of the architecture.
  • HouseAliciaAnother theatre family. Compared with Carmen and Gaudí on pitch, not on colour.
  • FlexFlex 6035A flexible system for a hall whose rows change. From the Flex family.
  • DesignFlameA designed seat, model 13107. For a room where the seat is seen before it is counted.
  • AuditoriumVirtusVirtus 13205. A hall seat from the catalogue, not a renamed copy.
  • OperaLyonLyon 13108. The family name points to the factory’s opera-house work.
  • CinemaHollywoodHollywood 5400 for the cinema. The pitch is longer than a lecture seat.
  • Wide cinemaMegaseatMegaseat 9042. A wider cinema seat. The family also has stadium models.
  • CinemaSmartSmart 13030. Another cinema line, with fixed and moving models.
  • UniversityAptumAptum 246 for a university tier. The family includes 247 and 248.
  • LectureDeltaDelta 430, designed with Josep Lluscà. For a hall where the arm is part of the room.
  • Writing seatTopTop 5036. A writing seat from the hall line, not the stadium line.
