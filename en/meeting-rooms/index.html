Meeting rooms: speech privacy and clear video meetings

Today’s meeting room is half in-person and half remote. If echo is high or the microphone is far, remote participants hear muddy sound; if the wall is weak, confidential discussions are heard in the corridor. At BoxTech O2mea we design and build meeting rooms that combine privacy, clarity and ease of use.

Consult on WhatsApp0541102224

How we approach meeting rooms

Speech privacy starts with wall, ceiling and door: a wall running to the structural slab, not just to the suspended ceiling, a well-sealed door, and treatment of HVAC paths that carry sound between rooms.

Inside the room we lower echo with absorptive ceilings and wall panels, because small rooms with glass and hard surfaces make video meetings tiring.

We then select a camera, microphones and loudspeakers sized to the room, a display readable from the last seat and a simple control panel that starts the meeting with one touch.

Checklist before you start

  • Is the seating format and its flexibility defined?
  • Is reverberation short enough for microphones and streaming?
  • Is HVAC background noise low?
  • Are camera positions and face lighting planned?
  • Does the hall need interpretation or division?
  • Is the control interface simple for the operating team?

Common mistakes we help you avoid

  • An untreated glass room that makes video meetings tiring
  • Walls stopping at the suspended ceiling and weakening privacy
  • A screen too small for the room depth
  • Complex systems without training

How to prepare your request

To speed up the study, prepare what you have: the architectural plan (PDF or CAD), building sections and clear heights, the expected audience size, the main uses in order of importance, photos of the existing site if any, and the target programme. The information need not be complete; helping you complete it is part of our work.

Frequently asked questions

Why can the meeting be heard in the corridor?

Usually because the wall stops at the suspended ceiling, because of the door, or through HVAC ducts. We treat those paths specifically.

Does glass weaken isolation?

Single glazing does; double or laminated glazing in sealed frames improves it a lot.

Are systems compatible with our meeting platform?

We select equipment compatible with your organisation’s platform.

Do you work in occupied offices?

Yes, minimising disruption to daily work.
