Scope

Six paths. One contract when you want it.

  1. 01

    Hall and theater design

    Capacity, sightlines, and function are settled before materials — for universities, government venues, and multi-use halls.

  2. 02

    Acoustic studies and isolation

    Echo, isolation, acoustic panels, and fabrics so speech stays clear from the first row to the last.

  3. 03

    Sound, image, and control

    Professional audio, screens and projection, and control rooms built to stay reliable in conference and performance.

  4. 04

    Stage and aisle lighting

    Architectural and stage lighting, including linear stair edging, specified per hall rather than from a generic catalogue.

  5. 05

    European theater seating

    Auditorium, cinema, and stadium seats in original materials, with warranty from three years and embroidery for official clients.

  6. 06

    Venue operation and events

    Operation, hire, and conference delivery — support for the contractor and the consultant through opening day.
